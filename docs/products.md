# Tahir Collection — Product Catalog Overview

A detailed look at our tubular stockinette product range.

## Cotton Tubular Stockinette

Soft, breathable, and skin-friendly. The standard choice for:
- Orthopedic casting (as a comfort liner)
- Wound care and bandaging
- General medical and protective use

## Nylon Tubular Stockinette

Lightweight and durable. Commonly used in:
- Prosthetics fabrication
- Orthotics
- Applications needing strength at low weight

## Perlon Tubular Stockinette

Strong and resilient. Suited to:
- Prosthetics
- Durable medical applications
- Industrial uses

## Fiberglass-Compatible Stockinette

Engineered to work with fiberglass casting systems:
- Orthopedic fiberglass casting workflows
- Modern casting environments

## Specifications

- **Size range:** 2 cm to 40 cm (approx. 0.75" to 15.75")
- **Grades:** medical grade and industrial grade
- **Customization:** OEM and custom specifications available
- **HS Code:** 6307.90
- **Packaging:** export-ready packaging available
