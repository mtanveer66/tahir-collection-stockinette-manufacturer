# Tahir Collection — Applications & Industries

Where our tubular stockinette is used.

## Orthopedic Casting

Cotton and fiberglass-compatible stockinette serve as the protective comfort layer under orthopedic casts, improving patient comfort and skin protection.

## Prosthetics & Orthotics

Nylon and perlon stockinette are used in the fabrication of prosthetic and orthotic devices, where strength and consistency matter.

## Wound Care & Bandaging

Soft cotton tubular stockinette is used in wound dressing and bandaging applications where skin-friendliness is essential.

## Lymphedema & Compression

Tubular stockinette is used as a component in compression and lymphedema management workflows.

## Garment & Apparel Manufacturing

Beyond medical use, tubular stockinette is used in garment and apparel manufacturing as a seamless knitted component.

## Distribution & Import

We supply distributors and importers serving any of the above markets — bulk, export-ready, with international shipping experience.
