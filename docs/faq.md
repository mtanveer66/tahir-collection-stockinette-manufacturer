# Tahir Collection — Frequently Asked Questions

## About the Product

**What is tubular stockinette?**
A seamless, tube-shaped knitted fabric used as a protective and comfort layer in orthopedic casting, prosthetics, wound care, and related applications.

**What materials do you manufacture?**
Cotton, nylon, perlon, and fiberglass-compatible tubular stockinette.

**What sizes do you offer?**
A full range from 2 cm to 40 cm (approximately 0.75" to 15.75").

**Is your stockinette medical grade?**
We produce both medical-grade and industrial-grade tubular stockinette.
